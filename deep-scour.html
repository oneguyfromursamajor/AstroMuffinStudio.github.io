Deep Scour Recovery

Deep Scour Recovery is a recovery tool from Astro Muffin Digital Labs, built to help recover what you thought was once lost. It is designed to search for files that were deleted, formatted, misplaced, damaged, or left behind on drives and storage devices.

AI may assist with parts of the technical process, but the goal stays human: reassurance, safety, clarity, and helping people get back the memories and files that matter to them.

Recovery

Bring Back What Matters

Deep Scour does its best to find recoverable photos, videos, documents, and other files from supported storage devices.

Validation

Check The Results

Recovered items can be checked, validated, and marked so users have a clearer idea of what worked, what failed, and what needs attention.

Honest Limits

Not Everything Can Be Saved

Some files may not be recoverable depending on damage, overwriting, formatting, prior cleanup tools, device failure, or how much new data has been written over the original space.

Recovery Disclaimer

Deep Scour cannot guarantee that every file can be recovered. Some data may be permanently damaged, overwritten, corrupted, or missing. The goal is to do everything possible to bring back what can still be found, while being clear and honest about what cannot be recovered.
