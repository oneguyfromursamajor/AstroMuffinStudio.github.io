Star Station Saga I

Bright Star Station

Go aboard the Bright Star Station with Alex, Rhea, and the crew as they make their way through cosmic trouble, strange misadventures, and the beginning of a much larger saga.

Bright Star Station is set on a once-derelict space station that has become a home for hundreds. It serves as a way station between galaxies, full of travelers, workers, odd corners, and stories waiting to unfold.

Development Status

Early Development

Episode 1 artwork is in progress. The first episode is being built from a large collection of individual art frames, approaching nearly 2,000 pieces of visual work before sound, music, voices, and final editing are added.

Timeline

Long-Term Project

Bright Star Station is a passion project expected to grow across the next three to five years as the art, sound, music, voice, editing, and production pipeline continue to develop.

Art Process

Human-Led

AI-assisted tools may help with parts of the visual workflow, but no final scene is simply AI output. Each scene is guided, edited, composited, and shaped by human imagination and hands-on direction.
