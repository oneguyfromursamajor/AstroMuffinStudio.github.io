About The Studio

Built for useful tools and impossible worlds.

Astro Muffin Studios is the home base for practical digital tools and creative worlds. Digital Labs focuses on software for recovery, privacy, organization, and safer everyday computing. Creative Works focuses on stories, games, animation, and worlds built for wonder.

Mission

AI clears the brush. Humans do the dreaming. Astro Muffin uses modern tools because the world moves forward, but the heart of the studio is still human imagination.

Independent Studio

Astro Muffin Studios is currently a small independent studio, built by one creator fueled by passion, coffee, and stubborn imagination. Many projects are in development. As the studio grows, the goal is to build a paid creative team, support good people with good work, and bring these worlds to life faster.
