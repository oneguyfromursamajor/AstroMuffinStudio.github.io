About Astro Muffin Studios

One studio. Two branches. Practical tools and creative worlds.

Astro Muffin Studios is the home base for two connected branches: Astro Muffin Digital and Astro Muffin Creative. Digital builds useful software for recovery, privacy, organization, and safer everyday computing. Creative builds stories, animation, games, characters, and worlds made for wonder.

Built From Testing and Imagination

I use my background in laboratory work, science, computers, and formal university training from different states to build products the same way I trust good lab work: test, test again, document what happened, and keep improving. That practical side is matched by the creative side of me that loves stories, music, art, travel, and the worlds people carry with them.

If I were a fantasy character, I would probably be a bard: someone drawn to stories, songs, strange roads, and the little details that make people feel alive. Astro Muffin Studios exists because both sides are real: the lab-minded builder and the storyteller.

Astro Muffin Digital

The Digital branch focuses on practical tools: Deep Scour Recovery, Phantom / Decoy Vault, Organizer, Secure Eraser, and future privacy utilities. The philosophy is simple: fair one-time pricing, no microtransactions, no pay-to-play, and respect for user privacy.

Astro Muffin Creative

The Creative branch is for art, stories, animation, games, trailers, character work, and future merch. Its lead project is Bright Star Station, Book I of A Star Station Saga.

Independent Studio

Astro Muffin Studios is currently a small independent studio built by one creator with a long project list, a stubborn imagination, and an unreasonable relationship with coffee. As the studio grows, the goal is to build a paid creative and technical team, support good people with good work, and bring these tools and worlds to life faster.

Mission

Build software that gives people more control over their digital lives, and build creative worlds that give people somewhere to dream. Practical tools. Creative worlds. A brighter tomorrow.
